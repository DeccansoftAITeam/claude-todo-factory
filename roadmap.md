# Roadmap

1. **Core list** — add, complete, delete, persist. *(shipped)*
2. **Focus & organization** — filter All/Active/Completed, a count of items left, clear completed, reorder.
3. **Editing** — edit a todo's title in place, undo delete.
4. **Polish & accessibility** — keyboard shortcuts, focus management, reduced motion, high contrast.
5. **Reliability** — graceful handling of corrupt or full storage, and e2e coverage of every flow.

## Triage guidance
- **ready-to-implement** — small, clear, user-visible, fits areas 2–5, and roughly under 300 lines with tests.
- **ready-to-spec** — fits the roadmap but needs design decisions (e.g. drag-and-drop reorder with persistence and keyboard support).
- **needs-info** — unclear expected behavior.
- **wait-to-implement** — non-goals in `vision.md` (accounts, sync, backend), or duplicates.
